### English

- Roster optimizer (OR-Tools CP-SAT) with an independently written rule validator: on 12 held-out months of a synthetic department it granted 62.8 % of wishes against 39.6 % for a simulated manual planner, with 0 instead of 5 hard-rule violations and 55.45 instead of 492.75 overtime hours; the validator agrees with a second checker on 71/71 published months and 300/300 random changes.
- Four ML components (absence forecast, preference learner, cover ranker, leave feasibility) evaluated against promotion rules written before the held-out year; none beat its simple baseline, so the baselines are served and the negative results are in the model cards.
- Two Claude-based agents behind safety gates and a hard budget: the intake agent reached 99.3 % against 25.0 % for the keyword stand-in but followed 1 of 9 planted instructions, so it is not served; the planner copilot passed all safety gates (0 followed instructions, 0 runs that changed anything but a proposal, 0 privacy findings in 130 runs) and is served for what-ifs and drafting only. All paid model calls together cost $0.17.
- Human-in-the-loop by construction: agents act with the signed-in user's permissions and have no publish or approve tool; hash-chained audit trail, durable timers on a demo clock, MCP servers, people replaced by aliases in everything sent to a model.
