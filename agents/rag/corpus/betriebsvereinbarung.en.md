This document is a **synthetic example** of a works agreement. It belongs to no real hospital and no real works council.

## [§ 1] Scope
(1) This agreement applies to the medical staff of the Klinik für Innere Medizin at Klinikum Musterstadt.
(2) It governs the use of DienstPilot to support duty scheduling. DienstPilot proposes; the responsible physician decides.

## [§ 2] No automatic publication
(1) A roster becomes visible only after the planner has approved and published it.
(2) The system may not publish a roster or give an approval. Assistant functions may create proposals that appear in the planner's inbox.

## [§ 3] Wishes and the request window
(1) The request window for a month lasts 14 days and ends before the plan is calculated.
(2) Wishes are inputs, not promises. Up to two wishes per person can be marked as important.
(3) Reasons for wishes are not recorded and not stored.

## [§ 4] Shift swaps
(1) A swap is offered only to colleagues who are qualified and can take the shift without breaking a rule.
(2) If all rules still hold for both people after the swap, the system confirms it itself (approval under the planner's policy); the planner is informed. Otherwise the planner decides and gives a reason.

## [§ 5] Sick calls and cover
(1) A sick report is stored only as absent with dates. No diagnosis or reason is recorded. Colleagues see only absent; the planner also sees the category.
(2) The cover search asks suitable colleagues one after another, each with a 15-minute deadline.
(3) Covers that cause overtime, involve an external locum or another department are decided by the planner.

## [§ 6] Changes to a published roster
(1) Only the planner changes a roster after publication, and must give a reason.
(2) All affected staff are notified.

## [§ 7] Log and co-determination
(1) Every change is kept in a tamper-evident log that oversight can read.
(2) The system does not rate individual people and makes no performance statements.
(3) Co-determination under § 87 (1) nos. 2 and 6 BetrVG remains unaffected.
