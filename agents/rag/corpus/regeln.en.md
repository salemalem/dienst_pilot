This summary is **synthetic**. It describes rule version 2026-1 of the fictional Klinik für Innere Medizin, Klinikum Musterstadt, as the prototype applies it. These are our own assumptions. No collective-agreement text is included; it is copyrighted and not reproduced here.

## [Rule 1] Rest period
(1) At least 11 hours of uninterrupted rest lie between two duties (rule min_rest_hours, modelled on § 5 ArbZG).
(2) An on-call duty (Bereitschaftsdienst) is followed by a free day (rule free_day_after_on_call).

## [Rule 2] Night shifts
(1) At most 3 night shifts in a row (rule max_consecutive_nights). In rule version 2021-1 it was still 4.
(2) Night shifts and on-call duty need the qualification nachtdienstfaehig. In on-call duty at least one person must be a specialist (Facharzt).

## [Rule 3] Weekends and on-call
(1) At most 2 weekends with duty per person and month (rule max_weekends_per_month).
(2) At most 4 in-hospital on-call duties per person and month (rule max_on_call_per_month) and at most 10 on-call periods at home (rule max_on_call_home_per_month).
(3) An on-call duty is credited with 18 hours (rule bereitschaft_credited_hours).

## [Rule 4] Contract hours
(1) Monthly hours lie between 80 % and 110 % of the contract hours (rule contract_hours_band).
(2) Falling below the band by giving a shift away sends the decision to the planner; an exchange for another shift keeps the hours.

## [Rule 5] Call duty
(1) Call duty (Rufbereitschaft) is covered by senior physicians (Oberärzte) (rule qualification_mix, shift ruf).
