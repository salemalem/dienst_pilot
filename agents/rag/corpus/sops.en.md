These procedures are **synthetic** and describe how the prototype models the process.

## [SOP-1] Sick call and cover search
(1) Anyone reporting sick gives only the dates from and to. There is no field for a diagnosis.
(2) For every shift in the period that has not begun, the cover search starts. The system checks who is qualified, off duty and legally permitted to take it, and orders colleagues by their earlier cover burden.
(3) The first person is asked and has 15 minutes. If they do not answer, the next is asked.
(4) If someone accepts, the system checks the roster again. If everything stays valid and no overtime arises, the planner's policy confirms the cover. Otherwise the planner decides.
(5) If the list is exhausted, the planner gets a decision with the ordered options and their consequences.

## [SOP-2] Shift swap
(1) Anyone who wants to give a shift away offers it. The offer goes only to colleagues who can take it without breaking a rule.
(2) If someone takes the offer, alone or in exchange for one of their own shifts, the system checks both rosters.
(3) If everything is valid and automatic approval is on, the swap is confirmed. Otherwise it appears with a reason in the planner's inbox (for example hours_band or rule_violation).

## [SOP-3] Request window
(1) The request window opens six weeks before the month and lasts 14 days.
(2) Anyone who has not answered 7 days and again 2 days before the end gets an in-app reminder. The planner can also remind manually at any time.
(3) After the window closes, the system calculates two to three valid variants. A run while the window is open is a trial run.

## [SOP-4] Month end
(1) As soon as a month is over, the system prepares the documentation: hours, overtime, rest periods, distribution of night, weekend and holiday duties, and the decision trail.
(2) The planner confirms the documentation with one click. That closes the month and releases the payroll export.

## [SOP-5] Change to a published roster
(1) The planner changes only future days and gives a reason.
(2) The system rejects a change that breaks a rule and names the rule.
(3) All affected people are notified, and the change is in the log.
